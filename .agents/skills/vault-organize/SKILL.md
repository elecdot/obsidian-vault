---
name: vault-organize
description: Organize captures, sources, activities, domains, and connections in this personal vault. Use when the author wants to retain, place, connect, refine, or retire material while preserving its purpose.
---

Read the relevant material before deciding where it belongs. Use
[the model](../../../CONTEXT.md), [relationships](../../../docs/metadata.md#relationships),
and [lifecycle meanings](../../../docs/metadata.md#lifecycle-semantics).
Scope the operation to the requested material and existing authorization.

Keep retention, placement, citation, and refinement as separate judgments.
An existing reference may be sufficient; a working explanation may stay in
its Activity or reading context. Extract an independently useful explanation
when it has a real purpose, retaining its source path. A relationship can be
recorded on a Capture without adding it to the entry's authored Guide.

Use [operations](../../../docs/operations.md) to inspect actual metadata and
locations. Mechanical findings do not decide the author's intended Domain
relationships or whether the content is worth retaining. Surface uncertain
interpretations; execute unambiguous changes already within the request.

Move or rename Notes through native Obsidian so it can update references.
From the terminal, use [vault-obsidian-cli](../vault-obsidian-cli/SKILL.md)
with the explicit target vault. If the native interface is unavailable,
preserve the files and report the unresolved move.
Do not overwrite importer-owned Sources with personal organization; put the
author's classifications and explanations in authored Notes.

Retired Domains retain their explanation and successor routes. Activity
states reflect explicit choices, not inactivity or age. Discard Captures only
when the author's request or adopted judgment establishes that decision.
After changes, inspect the affected references and report the resulting
locations and remaining questions.
