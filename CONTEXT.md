# Personal Knowledge Vault

A personal workspace for developing explanations, organizing knowledge, and
retaining useful thinking across learning, work, and daily life.

## Language

**Note**:
A unit of writing that preserves a question, explanation, interpretation, or
record and can be revisited, referenced, and developed.
_Avoid_: Concept card as a blanket name for all writing

**Concept Note**:
A Note devoted to a distinct, stable concept that needs independent reuse.
It provides an independently addressable explanation that can continue to
develop.
_Avoid_: Keyword placeholder, immutable final explanation

**Source**:
Material drawn on by a Note or used in an Activity, such as a paper, book,
course resource, webpage, conversation, or an earlier personal Note. It is
distinct from the interpretation that draws on it; an explanation can itself
serve as a Source for later writing.
_Avoid_: Merely related material, source-record file as the material's identity

**Activity**:
A course of learning or work around a specific object or goal, connecting
relevant Sources, Notes, and, when useful, progress information. It can be
chosen for future work before it has started.
_Avoid_: Domain, project as a blanket name for every learning context

**Domain**:
An enduring, evolving area of questions, concepts, and relationships.
Domains may overlap, and their organization develops with understanding.
_Avoid_: Exclusive folder, fixed syllabus

**Retired Domain**:
A Domain retained after an explicit decision to end its ongoing stewardship
or replace its organizing structure. A lapse in attention alone does not
make a Domain retired.
_Avoid_: Temporarily unfocused Domain, automatically obsolete member Notes

**Guide**:
A Note that provides an authored route into a Domain, Activity, or related
material by explaining important questions, relationships, and entry points.
_Avoid_: Exhaustive file listing, automatically generated knowledge structure

**Journal**:
A Note organized around a date or period, preserving experiences,
reflection, emerging thoughts, and connections to ongoing work.
_Avoid_: Mandatory activity report, project status record

**Capture**:
An intake record that preserves material, a question, or an emerging thought
while its longer-term use is still being considered.
_Avoid_: Mandatory refinement task, evidence of understood knowledge

**Refinement Candidate**:
A passage, question, or relationship identified as potentially worth further
development, with its context and the reason for considering it preserved.
_Avoid_: Committed task, confirmed conclusion

**Task**:
An action the author has chosen to pursue, bounded by an outcome whose
completion can be judged. Its completion state is maintained in one place.
_Avoid_: Refinement Candidate, an implied commitment from an unaccepted suggestion

**Workspace**:
An environment in which learning or work takes place, such as a code
repository or a specialized knowledge repository. An Activity may connect
to more than one Workspace.
_Avoid_: Activity, required vault folder
